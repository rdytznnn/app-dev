🐲 My Favorite Series: How to Train Your Dragon

"The bravest thing you can do is believe in yourself."

🌟 Overview
How to Train Your Dragon is an epic fantasy adventure about a Viking boy named Hiccup and a Night Fury dragon, Toothless. Their unlikely friendship changes the destiny of Berk and proves that understanding is stronger than fear.

🧙‍♂️ Main Characters

Character	Role

1.Hiccup	The clever and courageous Viking
2.Toothless	A loyal Night Fury and Hiccup’s best friend
3.Astrid	A fierce warrior and Hiccup’s partner
4.Stoick the Vast	Chief of Berk and Hiccup’s father

❤️ Why I Love This Series
The story is emotional and inspiring
It teaches friendship, courage, and acceptance
The music, animation, and visuals are breathtaking
